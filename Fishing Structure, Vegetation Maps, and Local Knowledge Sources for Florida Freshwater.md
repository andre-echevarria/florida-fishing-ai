# Fishing Structure, Vegetation Maps, and Local Knowledge Sources for Florida Freshwater

This document summarizes potential data sources for fishing structure, aquatic vegetation maps, and local knowledge relevant to developing an AI fishing prediction model for Florida freshwater bodies.

## Fishing Structure and Vegetation Maps:

**Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC):**
*   **GIS and Mapping - FWC (gis.myfwc.com):** This is a key resource. It provides a 


*   **GIS and Mapping Data Downloads:** This section provides access to various data and maps in different formats, viewable through ArcGIS.com.
*   **Fish Range Finder:** An application to identify fish ranges by name or county, which helps in understanding fish distribution.
*   **Florida Freshwater and Tidal Stream Fish Distribution Mapping: Phase I - Panhandle:** This project used GIS to generate potential habitat maps for 55 freshwater fish species in Florida's panhandle region. This is highly relevant for identifying fish-specific locations.
*   **Florida Stream Habitat Classification:** A statewide stream habitat classification GIS data layer that can help identify existing and potential habitats of aquatic species.
*   **Mapping Threats to Florida Freshwater Habitats:** This project assembled statewide data layers representing 10 uncorrelated individual threats to Florida's freshwater habitats and created a composite index of the threat data layers to determine relative condition of freshwater habitats within Florida. This could be useful for identifying less ideal fishing spots or understanding environmental pressures.

## Local Knowledge:

*   **Florida Fishing Spots Maps with GPS Coordinates (flfishingspots.com):** This website offers fishing spots maps and GPS coordinates for both inshore and offshore areas in Florida. While it mentions inshore, it could be a source for general hot spots and local knowledge, especially if it includes freshwater areas.
*   **GPS Nautical Charts (gpsnauticalcharts.com):** This site provides lake navigation features including fish attractor locations and fishing points, which are forms of local knowledge or known productive areas.

These resources will be vital for incorporating spatial data on fish habitats, structures, and local hot spots into the AI model.
